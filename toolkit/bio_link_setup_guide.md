## 4. How to Put the Link on Your TikTok Profile

2. Tap **Profile** > **Edit profile**.
3. Scroll down to the **Website** field.
4. Paste your link (e.g. `beacons.ai/tiktokplaygames` or your custom domain).
5. Update your bio text to one of the options above.
6. Tap **Save**.
