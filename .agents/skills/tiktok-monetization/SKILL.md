# TikTok Monetization Skill

This skill guides monetization strategies, revenue stream planning, and deal negotiation for TikTok creators.

## Key Monetization Channels

### 1. TikTok Creator Rewards Program
- **Eligibility:** 10k followers, 100k views/30d, 18+ years old, Personal Account, original 1min+ videos.
- **RPM Range:** $0.40 to $1.50+ per 1,000 qualified views.
- **Optimization Strategy:**
  - Length: Aim for 61–75 seconds (longer than 60s to qualify, but concise to maximize retention).
  - Retention Target: Aim for >30% completion rate and >70% at 3 seconds.
  - Topics: Original commentary, educational deep-dives, storytelling, high-value tutorials.

### 2. TikTok Shop for Creator / Affiliate
- **Eligibility:** 1,000 to 5,000 followers (regional dependent).
- **Commission Rates:** 10%–30% typical commission.
- **Workflows:**
  - Request free samples directly from sellers in the TikTok Shop Showcase.
  - Video structure: Hook -> Problem demonstration -> Product solution -> Social proof -> Call-to-action ("Click the orange cart below").
  - Target products with high sales velocity (1,000+ sold) and 15%+ commission.

### 3. Brand Sponsorships & UGC Deals
- **Pricing Guideline for 62k Followers:**
  - Standard Sponsored Post: $300 – $1,200 (based on 5%–10% average engagement).
  - Formula: `(Average Views / 1000) * $25–$45 CPM` + Usage Rights.
  - Spark Ad Authorization (30-day code): +30% to +50% markup.
  - Whitelisting / Dark Posting: +50% markup.
  - UGC (Content creation only, brand posts to their ads): $200 – $600 per video.

### 4. Digital Products & Email Funnels (High Margin)
- Use link-in-bio (custom web page, Stan Store, Beacons, Gumroad).
- Free lead magnet to capture emails (Beehiiv/Substack/ConvertKit).
- Paid digital assets: Notion templates, e-books, spreadsheets, presets ($15–$75).

## Deal Negotiation Checklist
1. Never quote one-off rates without asking for the scope: Deliverables, Exclusivity, Usage Rights (paid ads/organic), Whitelisting timeframe.
2. Provide a 3-tier package:
   - Tier 1: 1 Dedicated TikTok Video + link in bio (7 days).
   - Tier 2: 1 Dedicated Video + Spark Ad Code (30 days) + 1 Cross-post to IG Reels/Shorts.
   - Tier 3: 3-Video Series + Spark Ad Code (60 days) + Exclusivity in category (30 days).
