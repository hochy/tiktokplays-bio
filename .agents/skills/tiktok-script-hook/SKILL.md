# TikTok Script & Hook Engineering Skill

This skill provides frameworks for scripting 60s+ TikTok videos that satisfy the Creator Rewards Program threshold while sustaining viewer retention and driving conversion.

## The 60-75s High-Retention Architecture

```
[00:00 - 00:03] THE HOOK (Visual + Auditory)
- Curiosity gap, bold contrarian claim, or immediate pain-point demonstration.
- Pattern interrupt: movement, zoom, fast text overlay, or unexpected sound.

[00:03 - 00:15] THE STAKES / CONTEXT
- Why this matters right now.
- Frame the problem the viewer is suffering from or unaware of.

[00:15 - 00:45] CORE VALUE DELIVERY (Paced in 3 quick points)
- Point 1: Fast actionable tip.
- Point 2: Deeper insight / common mistake avoided.
- Point 3: The secret or counterintuitive nuance.
- Retention cues: B-roll cuts every 2.5-3.5 seconds; text keywords highlighted on screen.

[00:45 - 00:60] PROOF / RESOLUTION (Crossing the 60-second mark)
- Real-world example, before-and-after, or data demonstration.
- This ensures the video stays comfortably past 60s for monetization eligibility.

[00:60 - 00:70] CALL TO ACTION (Conversion Engine)
- For Affiliate: "I linked the exact one I use in the orange cart below before it sells out."
- For Lead Magnet: "Grab the full free checklist at the link in my bio."
- For Engagement: "Drop your take below: would you do X or Y?"
```

## Top 5 Hook Frameworks
1. **The Contrarian Truth:** "Stop doing [common practice]. Here is what actually happens when you do [alternative]..."
2. **The "Secret" Resource:** "Most people don't know this website/tool exists, but it literally saved me 10 hours this week..."
3. **The Loss Aversion Warning:** "If you have [target situation], you are losing money every day without realizing it..."
4. **The Step-by-Step Breakdown:** "I tested 10 different [things], and only one was actually worth it..."
5. **The Curiosity Loop:** "Do not buy [Product/Service] until you check this one hidden setting..."
