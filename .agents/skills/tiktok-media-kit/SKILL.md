# TikTok Media Kit Skill

Use this skill when preparing pitch decks, web media kits, or PDF one-pagers for brand sponsorships.

## Essential Components of a 62k Media Kit
1. **Header & Creator Bio:**
   - Channel handle, niche, 1-sentence value proposition ("Helping Gen Z master personal finance without the boring jargon").
   - Total follower count (62k) and monthly impression range.
2. **Audience Demographics:**
   - Top countries (e.g., US 65%, UK 15%, Canada 10%).
   - Age breakdown (e.g., 18–24: 45%, 25–34: 35%).
   - Gender split.
3. **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
   - Average views per video (last 30 days).
   - Average engagement rate (Likes + Comments + Shares / Views).
   - Total video views / reach.
4. **Content Pillars & Examples:**
   - 3–4 video case studies or screenshots of previous high-performing videos.
5. **Services & Rate Card Packages:**
   - Dedicated TikTok Video.
   - Integrated / 30s Mention.
   - Spark Ad Usage Rights (30 / 60 / 90 days).
   - Multi-platform bundle (TikTok + Reels + Shorts).
6. **Brand Contact & Terms:**
   - Professional business email, turnaround time (typically 5–7 business days), revisions policy (1 free round of minor edits).
